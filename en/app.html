Trips, stage by stage

Etappa is a planner for trips with more than one stage: home, a rental house, a hotel, a ferry, a flight. Deadlines, payments and country notes sit on the board – no ads, no tracking.

Etappa is currently in beta on TestFlight. The App Store launch will follow.

What Etappa can do

Trip as a stage board

On iPad the trip is a board, on iPhone it is a chain. Same trip, two layouts. Etappa keeps the next step in view – at most one task per stage.

Deadlines and payments

Etappa points out nights with no stay, overlapping stays, time clashes and overdue payments on its own. You enter payments with a due date and a currency; foreign currencies are converted with European Central Bank rates.

Country notes

A fixed rule pack covers notes such as a vignette (toll sticker), low-emission zone sticker, toll, ID, ferry height or winter tyres. Each note names a source.

A hint to check, not legal advice, and not a promise that the rule still applies.

Travel folder

You collect travel papers in the folder. The entries sync through iCloud, photos and notes stay on the device. An ID is stored without a number: only the kind, the expiry date and a first name.

Explore around your stay

Bakeries, restaurants, cafés, supermarkets, beaches and walks through Apple Maps. Ratings and payment methods stay on Apple's own place card; a tap opens it inside Etappa.

Plan together

You can share a single trip or your whole home through iCloud, for example with your family. Roles: owner, editor, viewer. Children are travellers, not editors. Only people you invite can see the trip.

Animated trip motifs

Each trip has a motif: coast, mountains, fjord or a landmark. Some motifs move a little. You can also pick the motif yourself.

Your data

Etappa shows no ads and does not track you. There is no language model and no automatic booking.

Your trip data stays on your device and, if you turn on iCloud, in your own iCloud account. The developer does not read it.

Location is used only if you turn on arrival notices, and it is not sent to the developer. Address search and drive time go to Apple Maps, without booking names.
