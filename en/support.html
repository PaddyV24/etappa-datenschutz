Frequently asked questions

How do I share a trip?

You can share a trip through iCloud. You decide who is an editor and who is view-only. A trip is only shared with people you invite.

How do we use Etappa as a family?

Whoever set up Etappa shares their home: Settings → Your Travel Defaults → Sharing and iCloud → "Share home …". You can send the invitation by message, for example. Anyone who opens the link sees the home profile and all trips in it, including future ones. Each person needs their own Apple ID with iCloud.

Why do I see "View only"?

You have been added to this trip as a viewer. Viewers see all of the trip's content but cannot change anything. Only the owner can make you an editor.

Where is my data?

Your trip data stays on your device and, if iCloud sync is turned on, additionally in your own iCloud account. There is no Etappa server that receives your trip data. More detail is in the privacy policy.

How do I report a bug?

The easiest way is the feedback feature inside the app – you can optionally attach a diagnostic log. In the TestFlight beta you can also use TestFlight's own screenshot feedback. Or email us at support@voelz-media.de.
